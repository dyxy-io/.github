# Security policy

The security of user funds is our top priority. We appreciate the work of security researchers who help keep DYXY safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Do not open a public issue for security problems.**

Report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub: open the **Security** tab of the affected repository and click **Report a vulnerability**. If the issue is not tied to a specific repository, report it in this one.

Please include:

- A description of the issue and its potential impact
- The affected product, network and, if relevant, contract address or URL
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- Your name or handle, if you'd like to be credited

## What happens next

1. We acknowledge your report as soon as possible.
2. We investigate and keep you updated on our progress.
3. Once the issue is fixed, we coordinate disclosure with you.

## Scope

In scope:

- The DYXY web app at [app.dyxy.io](https://app.dyxy.io)
- DYXY smart contracts, once published
- DYXY websites and documentation

Out of scope:

- Vulnerabilities in third-party protocols DYXY integrates (Hyperliquid, Aster, PancakeSwap, Uniswap, Aerodrome, StakeWise, Aave, Morpho, Kamino, Fluid and others). Please report those to the respective teams.
- Social engineering, phishing of DYXY users or staff, and physical attacks
- Denial of service and spam
- Issues in outdated browsers or wallets

## Safe harbor

We will not take legal action against researchers who act in good faith, avoid privacy violations and service disruption, don't exploit an issue beyond what is needed to demonstrate it, and give us reasonable time to fix it before disclosure.
